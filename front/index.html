Welcome to Expense Tracker

How to Use

1. Upload Your Statements

Start by uploading your bank statements or credit card transactions:

  • Go to the Upload page
  • Select your statement file (.xlsx, .xls, or .csv)
  • Choose the correct statement type
  • Click Process to import your transactions

2. Categorize Transactions

After uploading, organize your transactions:

  • Visit the Categorize page
  • Assign categories to uncategorized transactions
  • Use suggested categories based on transaction history
  • Exclude irrelevant transactions if needed
  • Submit your changes to save categorizations

Supported Statement Types

  • Master Statement
  • Bank Hapoalim Statement
  • Master Card Statement
